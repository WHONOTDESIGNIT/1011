After spending years in the beauty device industry, we've learned that the biggest gap between a brand owner and a finished product is not a lack of factories — it's the translation from drawing to reality. "You design it, we build it and box it" is how we put our service logic into practice. Here is how we break that sentence down into a workable process, step by step.

## Stage one: prototyping

Prototyping is the most critical step in the entire process, and the one where most brand owners feel the least confident. The reason is simple: the distance between a drawing and a physical object is almost always bigger than you expect.

### Custom housing color: your brand deserves its own signature color

The biggest problem with public-mold IPL hair removal devices is that they all look the same. Black, white, pink — a handful of fixed colors cycled over and over, until consumers can't tell one product from another at a glance on the shelf.

We offer custom housing color services. Not picking from a fixed color card, but developing a signature color based on your brand identity and target audience preferences. Send us a Pantone number, and we'll reproduce that color accurately on the product housing. Or combine our existing surface finishing processes — matte, glossy, gradient — to find the texture that best fits your brand personality, and make your device stand out visually among the many similar IPL hair removal devices on the market.

This service moves you from the "anyone could have made this" category into the "only you could have made this" territory. Subtle differences in color often determine whether a consumer remembers "a hair removal device" or "your hair removal device."

### Logo printing: put your brand identity where it belongs

Once the color is decided, the next step is the logo. This is not as simple as sending a vector file to a factory. How the logo is applied affects the overall texture, durability, and brand impression of the product.

We offer two logo application methods: digital printing and screen/tampo printing. Digital printing is more economical and efficient, ideal for small-batch prototype samples. Screen/tampo printing suits colorful logos or designs with multiple color layers — higher color fidelity and crisp edges, ideal for bulk orders.

Either way, we execute strictly according to your brand VI files. The AI or CDR files you send are the standard; we don't do redesigns.

### Carton printing: packaging is the first impression

The carton is the first time a consumer touches your brand. A box with the wrong texture, blurry printing, or a flimsy structure will immediately downgrade how users judge your product's value.

We provide complete carton printing services — box structure, paper selection, printing processes, and surface finishing (lamination, hot foil stamping, UV coating, embossing) — all executed according to your design files. Every detail of the packaging should communicate your brand.

### Accessory checklist: everything users see when they unbox

A complete home-use IPL hair removal device is more than the device itself. When users open the box, they also see: the instruction manual, safety goggles, a shaver, a power adapter... The material, color, printing, and quality standard of every accessory shape the overall impression of your product.

We confirm the specifications and samples of all accessories at the prototyping stage — we never scramble to assemble them at mass production time. Whether the unboxing experience feels "premium" is decided at the prototyping stage.

## Stage two: confirm the minimum order quantity

Traditional OEM factories typically set minimum order quantities (MOQs) of 3,000 units, 5,000 units, or even higher. Behind that number is a logic: factories use large-scale production to amortize fixed costs.

But that logic doesn't fit every brand. Especially when a new brand or a new category enters the market, 3,000 units of inventory means hundreds of thousands or even millions of dollars tied up in stock. If it sells well, great — but what if it doesn't?

iShine's MOQ is just 100 units. It's a number that lets you validate the market without putting excessive financial pressure on your brand. Take 100 units first to test channel response, collect user feedback, and verify the real market performance of your product — whether it's an IPL hair removal device or any other beauty device. Once you confirm it works, scale up your order.

This isn't "lowering the barrier to entry" — it's "respecting the laws of business." No one can be 100% sure a product will sell out before launch — so validate with the smallest cost, not gamble with the biggest risk.

## Stage three: confirm branding and packaging

Once the MOQ is decided, we move into final confirmation of brand identity and packaging. This stage covers four core items:

- **Logo position and size** — finalize the position, size, and application process for the logo on the device.
- **Carton structure** — confirm the box style, material, printing process, and opening/closing structure.
- **Manual and printed materials** — finalize the manual content and layout, and confirm the specifications and branding of accessories such as safety goggles and the shaver.
- **Carton loading plan** — decide how the device, accessories, manual, and boxes fit inside the shipping carton, ensuring transport safety and optimized logistics costs.

Once these are confirmed, your product enters final production scheduling.

## Stage four: delivery

After prototyping approval, plan confirmation, and production scheduling, formal production begins. Delivery time depends on product complexity and the depth of customization.

For a standard custom order (including color customization, logo printing, packaging printing, and accessory configuration), the lead time is **20 to 35 working days**, counted from sample confirmation and deposit payment.

This includes: raw material sourcing, injection molding, surface finishing, logo printing, electronic assembly, full-unit testing, carton printing, accessory production, final assembly, and boxing for shipment. Every step has clear standards and traceable milestones.

If you need to rush, we can compress the schedule depending on the situation, but this usually involves cost adjustments and needs to be discussed in advance.

## All you need to do is send us your design

Many brand owners assume that working with a factory is a long, complicated process: endless back-and-forth, unforeseen obstacles, and problems that never stop coming.

In reality, if the process is designed clearly enough, it can be simple:

1. You send us your design (or concept idea).
2. We assess structural feasibility and give initial feedback.
3. We complete prototyping and customization (color, logo, packaging, accessories).
4. You approve the sample and the plan.
5. We run small-batch production starting at 100 units.
6. Within 20 to 35 working days, your product is boxed and handed to the logistics carrier.

You don't need to manage communication across the supply chain, and you don't need to coordinate separately with mold factories, injection molding plants, printing houses, packaging plants, or assembly lines — we handle all coordination for you. You only confirm the result.

That is what "You Design It, We Build It, Box It" really means: you focus on your brand and design, and we turn your product from a drawing into a product that sits on real shelves.
