# IPL Aftercare: What to Do After Each Session (Shaving, Skincare, Sun)

What you do after IPL hair removal decides most of your results and almost all of your side effects. Cool the skin, keep skincare minimal, avoid heat and friction, and protect treated areas from the sun. Shaving between sessions is fine — in fact it's required — but wait until the skin has settled, and never wax or tweeze during a treatment course. That's the whole routine in one paragraph; the detail below is why each step exists.

I'm Eric, co-founder and CEO of iShine. We've written the instruction manuals (IFUs) that ship inside IPL boxes for over 15 years. Aftercare is the chapter we spend the most time on — because it's the chapter that determines how many after-sales calls a brand receives.

## The Short Answer: 4 Steps After Every Session

1. **Cool and moisturize** — a cool towel for a short while, then a simple fragrance-free moisturizer.
2. **Skip actives** — no exfoliating acids, retinol, or fragrance on treated skin for at least 24 hours.
3. **Avoid heat and friction** — lukewarm showers, no saunas, no tight clothing over treated areas that day.
4. **Sun protection** — broad-spectrum SPF 30+ on any treated skin that will see daylight.

## Why Aftercare Exists: The Two Danger Lines

In the years we've spent making devices, safety problems come from two sources. One: flashing light onto skin that's already inflamed or sensitive. Two: piling more irritation on *after* the session — sun, heat, friction, and aggressive skincare all count. Aftercare holds the skin barrier steady and blocks the inflammation you can avoid. Hold those two lines and most of the trouble never shows up.

## Shaving After IPL: When and How

**Yes, you shave between sessions — it's part of the protocol.** IPL needs the hair root in the follicle, so shaving (which removes only the surface hair) is the correct prep for each session. Waxing and tweezing pull the root out, leaving empty follicles that the light can't work on. This is the most common aftercare question we get from users — "can I shave after IPL?" — and the answer is yes, with timing. Brand-side aftercare guides from major home IPL makers give the same guidance: shave, don't epilate, between treatments ([SmoothSkin](https://smoothskin.com/blogs/blog/what-to-do-before-ipl-hair-removal)).

The timing rules:

- **Before a session:** shave 12–24 hours ahead, not immediately before — fresh shave irritation plus a flash is double stress on the skin.
- **After a session:** wait until redness has settled, usually 24–48 hours. Shaving irritated skin adds friction exactly where the skin is trying to recover.
- **Between sessions:** shave as needed for the treated area to stay smooth. This is normal and expected.

One caution: don't shave with an aggressive blade or electric trimmer pressure on freshly treated skin. Gentle strokes, clean blade, no dragging.

## Skin Care After IPL: What to Use, What to Skip

The 24–48 hours after a session is a "skin barrier first" window. Post-IPL skincare routines from dermatology-led sources follow the same pattern — calm, protect, and don't stack actives ([Trynoha post-IPL routine](https://trynoha.com/blogs/news/post-ipl-skincare-routine-what-to-do-after-each-session)). The rules are simple.

**Use:**
- Gentle, fragrance-free moisturizer
- Broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen (the AAD's standard — [how to choose the right sunscreen](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/sun-protection/shade-clothing-sunscreen/choosing-right-sunscreen))
- Cool water for washing; lukewarm showers

**Skip (at least 24h, ideally 48h):**
- Exfoliating acids (AHAs/BHAs) on the treated area
- Retinoids and vitamin C serums — no buffer period needed if you simply pause them
- Fragranced body lotions, essential oils, or "active" creams
- Tight workout clothing that rubs treated legs or arms

## Sun: The Most-Underrated Aftercare Step

UV worsens redness and is the main driver of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation — the dark patches people fear most, especially on darker skin tones. Treated skin is temporarily more vulnerable because the session has already delivered heat and mild inflammation.

So: any treated area that will see sun needs coverage. This is not just a summer rule. Indoor UV through windows, and vacations in sunny regions, count too. Broad-spectrum SPF 30+ is the baseline. If you're treating your face, include SPF in your morning routine every day of the treatment course.

## When to Continue, When to Stop

Aftercare judgment is a two-question test:

- **Is the reaction getting lighter?** Normal redness and warmth should fade within hours. Continue as planned.
- **Is the skin broken?** Blisters, crusting, or deepening color over days mean stop — don't flash the same area until the skin is fully back to normal.

Normal: mild redness fading in hours, quick warmth, follicle bumps settling in a day or two.
Stop signals: burning pain during flash, worsening redness, blisters, crusting, color deepening over days. For the full normal-versus-warning comparison, see [IPL side effects in detail](/blog/32-ipl-hair-removal-side-effects-what-is-normal).

## The 4-Week Reality Check

Results are not immediate. Hair shedding happens 1–2 weeks after treatment, and visible reduction builds over 4–8 sessions with proper spacing (typically every 1–2 weeks per area, per the [safe at-home protocol](/blog/29-safe-ipl-hair-removal-at-home-protocol)). Treating more often than the schedule allows doesn't speed results — it stacks irritation. Consistency beats intensity.

## FAQ

### Can I shave after IPL?

Yes — after the redness settles (usually 24–48 hours). Shaving between sessions is required to keep the hair root available for the next treatment. Don't wax or tweeze during a treatment course.

### How soon after IPL can I shower?

You can shower right away with lukewarm water, but avoid hot water, saunas, and steam for the rest of the day. Pat the skin dry; don't rub.

### What skincare should I avoid after IPL?

Skip exfoliating acids, retinoids, vitamin C serums, and fragranced products on treated skin for 24–48 hours. Use a gentle fragrance-free moisturizer and SPF 30+.

### Why does my skin feel dry after IPL?

The flash temporarily disrupts the skin barrier and warms the surface. Mild dryness and itching are common and improve quickly with a simple moisturizer. If dryness worsens or the skin breaks, stop and reassess.
