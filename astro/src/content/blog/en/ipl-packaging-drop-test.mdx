**Short answer:** the pack has to carry a rigid device through a courier network, and it stays small because freight is charged on volume. The warnings block eats the panel — it comes out bigger than the logo. Those are the inputs the structural brief is built from.

## The contents drive the design

An IPL device is a rigid body with a lamp window, a power supply and a cable, and when one is dropped, the failure that comes back is a dead device or a cracked window.

| Input the converter needs | Why |
|---|---|
| Device weight and exact dimensions | Determines wall grade and internal fitment |
| Drop height and orientation | Set by the drop sequence the courier network uses |
| Master carton count | Sets the outer case strength and the pallet pattern |
| Market label text | The warnings block drives panel size |

Ask for all four before quoting.

## CBM is a design variable

Freight is charged on volume, so the master carton sits inside the cost model. Whether a carton takes one more layer on a pallet can matter more over a season than the price difference between two board grades, and a box that passes every drop test can still cost you that layer.

The number to compute is the volume the forwarder measures at the warehouse. It runs a little higher than the drawing predicts, and how much higher we take from their last measurement, so the figure is only as good as that.

## The warnings block is the hard part

Every market has its own required statements, and the block has to fit beside a logo, a model name, a barcode and whatever the retailer insists on. Whichever market's text runs longest sets the panel, which is why the label text is one of the four inputs above.

## Materials that survive a marketplace

A device sold online travels in a mailer, then a van, then a doorstep. Recycled board works at the right wall grade, and it needs a drop test behind it.

## The drop test covers the structure

We specify the packaging with the converter, and we hold the carton dimensions that the freight calculation uses. The pack that ships has been through the drop test. Printing and brand design sit outside that scope, and what we certify is a structural design we have tested.

## The converter's question

A converter who quotes a structural change will be asked why the board grade is what it is, and the answer is a drop test. Whose drop sequence that test uses is worth settling early.

Bring the failure mode: a dead device or a cracked lamp window.

The cube arithmetic goes into the same conversation: the volume the forwarder measured, and what the pallet actually held.

## The question that separates a quote from a design

Ask what the master carton has to hold on a pallet, and what the forwarder measured last time.

## The quote and the print questions

Quote the pack and the master carton together, and say which drop test the design assumes, because that assumption is what the price rests on. The weight you want includes the charger and the cable: the corner drop lands on it. Which market's warnings block runs longest is the one to chase down before the plate goes out, since it sets the panel.

## The drop-test standards

- [ISTA](https://www.ista.org/) — the packaging test sequences used for parcel and LTL distribution.
- [Materials, 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41010207/) — measured corrugated-board damage under vibration and handling.
- [IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations](https://www.iata.org/en/programs/cargo/dgr/) — the air-freight rules that classify a device with a lithium cell.

## FAQ

**What does an IPL box have to protect?**

A rigid body: a lamp window, a power supply, a cable. Whether a drop failed shows up in the window or in whether the unit powers on.

**What does a converter need before quoting an IPL pack?**

The device weight and exact dimensions, the drop height and orientation, the master carton count, and the market label text. For our own platforms the drop test runs at one metre, with vibration and compression beside it, and an external packaging lab runs the sequence.

**Why does carton volume change the cost?**

Because the forwarder bills the volume, the master carton is part of the cost. Compute the volume they measure at the warehouse.

**Which market's warnings text sets the panel layout?**

Whichever market's text runs longest. The block has to fit beside a logo, a model name, a barcode and whatever the retailer insists on.
