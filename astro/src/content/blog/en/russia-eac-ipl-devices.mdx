**Short answer:** an IPL device entering Russia and the Eurasian customs union is assessed against two technical regulations, low voltage and EMC, plus the hazardous substances regulation, and marked EAC. The rule that stops most first projects is not the testing. The applicant has to be a legal entity registered inside the union.

## EAC is a union mark, not a Russian one

The mark covers five member states, and the technical regulations are union law rather than national law. That is the good news: one assessment, one mark, five markets.

The less good news is that the structure is not one document. Three regulations usually matter for a mains-powered beauty appliance:

| Regulation | Scope | What it means in practice |
|---|---|---|
| TR CU 004/2011 | Low-voltage equipment safety | The electrical safety basis for the appliance and its adapter |
| TR CU 020/2011 | Electromagnetic compatibility | Emission and immunity, in the same family as EMC elsewhere |
| TR EAEU 037/2016 | Hazardous substances | The union's restriction list, comparable to RoHS |

A device with a radio module adds another assessment, and the union's treatment of radio equipment has been moving. Confirm that scope with the certification body rather than assuming it follows the EMC file.

## Declaration or certificate, decided by the list

The union uses two conformity forms: a declaration, registered by the applicant, and a certificate, issued by an accredited body. Which one applies is determined by the lists inside each technical regulation, and the two lists are specific by product type.

Nobody chooses the easier form. A supplier who says "we will do a declaration" without checking the list has not read the regulation.

The practical difference is real:

- **A declaration** is registered by the applicant, on the applicant's responsibility, and the evidence stays with them.
- **A certificate** involves an accredited body, a test report it accepts, and in some schemes a production analysis.

Both have a validity period. Both require the EAC mark. Neither one is permanent, and renewal is a project the applicant has to plan for.

## The applicant rule

This is the sentence to read twice: the applicant must be a legal entity or an individual entrepreneur registered in a member state of the union.

A manufacturer in China cannot be the applicant. Not with an agent's stamp, not with a power of attorney. The declaration or certificate is issued to a resident entity.

Three consequences follow, and they mirror every other market covered on this site:

- **The resident entity carries the responsibility**, including for market surveillance questions after the sale.
- **The label reflects that entity**, alongside the manufacturer.
- **The commercial relationship matters more than the paperwork**, because the entity's name is on the declaration for its whole validity period.

This is the same structural position as the EU responsible person, the Japanese importer and the Australian sponsor. The name changes; the shape does not.

## What the factory supplies

| Document | Detail | Where it goes wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Technical description | Construction, ratings, components, adapter and cell specification | Written for CE, with no Russian-language summary |
| Test reports | Safety and EMC to recognised standards, on the final configuration | Report predates the adapter change |
| Component certificates | Cells, adapters, critical parts | Component named by trade name only |
| Manual and label, in Russian | Full instructions, warnings, EAC mark placement | Machine-translated manual with the wrong warning wording |
| Production site information | Factory details for the file | Site changed after the sample was approved |

The Russian-language manual is where projects lose the most time, and it is not a translation exercise in the ordinary sense. Warnings, contraindications and the intended-use statement are regulated text, and a buyer's own translator will often rewrite them rather than accept a literal rendering.

## What has changed, and why you should ask twice

The procedural position in this market has been adjusted repeatedly in recent years, including simplified arrangements adopted under pressure and extended more than once. Some of those arrangements affect which documents a body will accept, and some affect what happens at the border.

That is why this article deliberately gives you the structure and not a fixed procedure. Before a project is quoted, ask the resident certification body two questions in writing: which conformity form applies to this product today, and which documents will they accept from a foreign manufacturer. The answers have moved often enough that a two-year-old blog post is worse than no post.

## The label

```
EAC mark:          <placed per the regulation>
Applicant:         <resident entity, address>
Manufacturer:      <factory legal entity, country>
Model:             <model code used in the assessment>
Rating:            <V, Hz, W>
Instructions:      <Russian-language manual supplied with each unit>
```

The instructions line is part of the label requirement in practice. A unit that ships without the Russian-language manual is not compliant, even with the mark correctly applied.
