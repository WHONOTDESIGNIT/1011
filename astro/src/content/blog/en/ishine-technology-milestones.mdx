## Why the Order Matters

A brand that shows up today and says "we make IPL devices" is not telling you much. The order of that timeline above is the more honest signal. We started with a single platform in 2021, added professional-grade hardware in 2022, introduced contact cooling in 2024, and layered in sensing and display technology in 2025. Each step built on the last, which is how an IPL device factory is supposed to work — you validate one architecture, then extend it.

## The Technologies That Define Each Platform

### Dual-Airflow Cooling Structure (Lumi, 2021)

Our first differentiator was not skin cooling — it was protecting the optical filter. The [Lumi](/products/lumi) uses a dual-airflow thermal management system: independent airflow channels draw heat away from the precision optical filter, the sealed airflow path keeps dust out and prevents abrasion, and the whole design keeps spectral output stable across the device's life. That was the engineering bet we made first, before anything else.

### Metal Cooling Tip + Safety Lock (Themis, 2022)

The [Themis](/products/themis) moved us into professional-grade territory: a metal cooling tip for contact comfort and a safety lock to prevent accidental flash. It also introduced skin rejuvenation as part of the treatment stack.

### Sapphire Ice Cooling (Venus, 2024)

The [Venus](/products/venus) brought sapphire contact cooling — a cold window against the skin rather than a fan inside the chassis. This is the comfort technology that keeps people actually finishing a 12-week course. We cover the contact-cooling hardware separately on the [cooling system page](/components/cooling-system).

### Dual-Lamp Series Circuit (Venus Pro, before Dec 2026)

The Venus Pro upgrade replaces a parallel lamp circuit with a **dual-lamp series circuit**. Compared with parallel designs, the equivalent resistance is four times higher, which cuts capacitor charging current to one quarter and extends pulse duration to 5–10 ms. The result is gentler energy release — more comfortable, easier on sensitive skin. It is the next step in the [Venus](/products/venus) line.

### Skin-Tone Sensor + IML Display (Lumi 2, 2025)

The [Lumi 2](/products/lumi-2) added automatic skin-tone detection — a safety feature that keeps darker skin from receiving too much energy — plus an IML (in-mold labeling) display built directly into the housing, which is more scratch-resistant and lets us keep the profile thin.

| UKCA | 2024 | — |

A note on FDA wording, because it matters more than most people think: a device that goes through the FDA 510(k) pathway is **cleared**, not "approved." "Approved" is reserved for the PMA pathway used by higher-risk devices. When a seller calls a home IPL device "FDA approved," that is loose language. Ours is FDA-cleared under K222537, and the number is searchable in the [FDA 510(k) database](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfPMN/pmn.cfm). We also hold FDA establishment registration 3021338880 and Canadian MDEL 177237.
