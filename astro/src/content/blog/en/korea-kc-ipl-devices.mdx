**Short answer:** Korea has three KC tiers, not one. An electrical appliance is either safety certified, safety confirmed, or handled by supplier conformity confirmation, and the tier decides whether a designated body tests the device or the supplier declares it. An app-connected handset also needs the radio approval, which is a second filing with a different scope.

## The tier is the whole conversation

Most manufacturers arrive with a question about "the KC test". The first answer they need is that there may be no test at their tier, or that the testing is done by a different kind of body.

| Tier | Who assesses | Evidence held | Typical shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| Safety certification | A designated certification body | Certificate issued by that body | Full type testing, factory inspection in some cases |
| Safety confirmation | A designated testing laboratory | Test report, registered | Testing without the certification body step |
| Supplier conformity confirmation | The supplier | Self-declared conformity plus internal test evidence | Declaration, with the file available on request |

Which tier applies is decided by the product category list, and the list is specific. A home IPL device is not a category that a supplier can assign to itself, and the tier does not follow the price, the power or the intended market. Confirm it against the current list with the lab or the Korean agent before planning the project.

That single check determines whether the project takes weeks or months, and whether a factory inspection is in scope.

## Who is allowed to apply

This is the part that stops projects, and it is the same shape as Japan and the EU.

The applicant is the business operator doing business in Korea: the manufacturer established there, or the importer. A manufacturer outside Korea works through a Korean agent, and the agent's details appear in the registration.

Three consequences:

- **The mark carries the applicant's identification**, so a Chinese factory address on the product is not the registration.
- **The applicant answers to the authority**, including for post-market issues.
- **The agent relationship is commercial, not administrative.** Agents price the responsibility, and they should, because it stays with them.

A brand with no Korean entity therefore needs an agent before it needs a label.

## The radio layer, for app-connected handsets

If the device has Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, the electrical filing is only half of it. Radio equipment carries its own conformity registration under a separate act, with its own testing and its own mark alongside KC.

Two things follow that catch people mid-project:

- **The radio approval tests the module, the antenna configuration and the output**, so a module swap after approval is a re-filing.
- **The radio approval is issued against the final configuration.** A handset that gains an app-controlled feature late in the design cycle goes back through it.

A cordless handset with a Bluetooth module and a phone app is three compliance objects in one box: the appliance, the radio, and the software that talks to it.

## What the agent will ask the factory for

| Document | Detail | Common gap |
|---|---|---|
| Product specification and ratings | Model, voltage, frequency, power, and the full model matrix | Family declared, but variants differ in the cell or the adapter |
| Test reports | Safety and EMC, to the standards applied | Report on an earlier hardware revision |
| Radio test data, where applicable | Module approval, antenna data, output power | Module approved in another product configuration |
| Label artwork | KC mark, applicant identification, ratings, and the radio mark | Artwork printed before the agent was identified |
| Component list | Cells, adapters, critical components with ratings | Trade names only, no ratings or certificates |
| Factory information | Production site, quality system evidence | Site changed between prototype and mass production |

The label artwork row is the same trap as every other market. The applicant's identity is not known until the agent is chosen, and the artwork cannot be finalised before that.

## The order of operations that works

1. Confirm the tier against the current category list, in writing.
2. Engage the Korean agent, who becomes the applicant or the applicant's representative.
3. Test to the standards the tier requires, on the final configuration.
4. File the radio registration if there is a radio.
5. Update label artwork with the applicant and both marks, then print.
6. Keep the file in a form the agent can produce on request.

Steps one and four are the ones that get skipped, and both are expensive to fix after tooling.
